# Master's thesis

I want to build a website to report the results of my master's thesis in a more interactive way. My master's thesis is on the effects of droughts and floods on the indian labor market. This is how I visualize the website: when you first arrive, you have some sunlight and soil, as you scroll down, the sunlight/heat gets more intense and you can see the soil getting more down, then you continue scrolling and some clods quickly arrive and as you scroll even longer it starts pouring rain intensely. Then the rain stops and the research question appears: "What are the effects of droughst and floods on informality in the indian labor market?". and the menu appears and you can navigate through different sections: Background (literature review), Methodology, Results
